# Hold the Send (holdthesend.com)

AI drafts dealership replies. Humans send. Hold the Send is a checked reply desk for car-dealership sales reps and BDC agents.

- A rep pastes a lead's message in and gets a draft built only from facts the rep supplied (price sheet, notes, availability).
- A check step holds any draft that invents a price, payment, discount, trade value, promise, or cash/gift, and shows the reason. Money asks are flagged for a manager.
- From 9 PM to 8 AM the rep must type why before copying.
- Phone numbers, emails, SSNs, DOBs, account numbers and street addresses are masked before the draft goes to the AI.
- A person always reads and presses Send. Nothing sends automatically. It is NOT a chatbot and is the wrong tool for stores that want unattended replies.
- Works next to the existing CRM or Page inbox: paste the lead in, copy the checked reply out.
- Aim: a booked store visit, with fewer rewrites and no wrong-price slips in writing.
