# Target market

Primary: individual sales reps and BDC / internet-sales agents at franchised and independent car dealerships (US) who answer internet leads, Marketplace and Messenger inquiries, including after hours.

Secondary (buyers): dealer principals / owners, general sales managers, internet or BDC managers, and digital-marketing / fixed-ops-adjacent managers at single-store and small dealer groups (1-10 rooftops) who carry the risk of wrong prices or promises in writing.

Good signals: titles like BDC Manager, Internet Sales Manager, BDC Agent, Sales Consultant, General Sales Manager, Dealer Principal; stores that use Facebook Marketplace/Messenger leads; worried about AI chatbots promising prices.

Exclude: vendors, agencies and software companies selling to dealers; large groups wanting autonomous chatbots; non-dealership auto businesses.
